# Union Budget 2026–27 Analysis

**Objective:** To analyze the 2026–27 Indian Union Budget allocations, identifying top-funded ministries, high-growth schemes, and key fiscal priorities.

**Data:** Union Budget 2026–27 expenditure datasets (raw and cleaned CSV files sourced from the Ministry of Finance).

**Methodology:** Built a structured SQL database and executed analytical queries using aggregations and window functions to extract ministry-level allocations and year-over-year scheme growth trends.

**Outputs:** Query results exported as CSV files for transparency and reproducibility.

**Key Findings:**

- **Top Ministries:** The Department of Rural Development received the highest allocation (₹2,28,480 Cr), followed by Drinking Water and Sanitation (₹74,862 Cr) and School Education (₹63,010 Cr).

- **Flagship Schemes:** Viksit Bharat-GRAM (₹95,692 Cr) and Jal Jeevan Mission (₹67,670 Cr) dominate scheme-wise funding, highlighting a strong policy focus on rural livelihoods and infrastructure.

- **Growth Priorities:** The Irrigation Census and Flood Management (FMBAP) schemes recorded the highest year-over-year growth in allocations (100% and 77.1%, respectively).
